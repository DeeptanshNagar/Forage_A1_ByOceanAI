# Approved Supplier Commercial Terms

Procurement Department, document PROC-TERMS-07, valid from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025.

## 1. General conditions

All catalogue prices are quoted in USD and exclude taxes, duties, freight and agency fees. Prices are valid for 90 days from the date of a quotation unless stated otherwise below. Orders are placed through the purchase order (PO) system only; verbal orders are not accepted.

## 2. Purchase order approval limits

Purchase orders are approved according to their total value: up to 5,000 USD by the technical superintendent, from 5,000 USD to 25,000 USD by the fleet manager, and above 25,000 USD by the technical director. The assistant or any superintendent may not split an order to stay below an approval limit.

## 3. Marisupply GmbH

Payment terms are 30 days from invoice date. Currency is USD. The minimum order value is 500 USD. Volume discount: 3% on orders above 10,000 USD and 6% on orders above 25,000 USD. Cancellation after order confirmation incurs a fee of 10% of the order value.

## 4. Oceanic Parts Co

Payment terms are 45 days from invoice date. Currency is USD. The minimum order value is 250 USD. Volume discount: 5% on orders above 15,000 USD. Prices are valid for 60 days from quotation. Cancellation is free of charge up to 48 hours after order confirmation, and 15% of the order value after that.

## 5. Baltic Marine Spares

Payment terms are 30 days from invoice date. Currency is USD. The minimum order value is 1,000 USD. Volume discount: 4% on orders above 20,000 USD. Cancellation after dispatch is not possible; before dispatch a fee of 5% applies.

## 6. Kestrel Engineering

Payment terms are 60 days from invoice date. Currency is USD. There is no minimum order value. Volume discount: 2% on orders above 5,000 USD and 5% on orders above 30,000 USD. Cancellation after order confirmation incurs a fee of 12% of the order value.

## 7. Harbour Tech Ltd

Payment terms are 14 days from invoice date with a 1.5% early payment discount if paid within 7 days. Currency is USD. The minimum order value is 300 USD. No volume discount is offered. Cancellation after order confirmation incurs a fee of 8% of the order value.

## 8. Applying discounts

Volume discounts apply to the order value of a single purchase order placed with one supplier, calculated from catalogue unit prices multiplied by quantities, before taxes and freight. Only the highest applicable discount tier applies; tiers are not cumulative.

## 9. Price disputes

Price discrepancies between an invoice and the confirmed purchase order must be reported to the supplier within 14 days of the invoice date. The procurement department handles all negotiations with suppliers.
