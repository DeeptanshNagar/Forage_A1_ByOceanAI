# Harbourline Shipping Ltd - Grievance and Disciplinary Procedure

Document HR-POL-03, version 2.1, effective 1 March 2025. Applies to all shore-based employees.

## 1. Scope

This procedure explains how employees can raise concerns about their work (grievances) and how the company handles misconduct (discipline). It does not apply during the probationary period of 6 months, during which a shortened process applies.

## 2. Informal resolution

Employees are encouraged to raise concerns informally with their line manager first. Most issues should be resolved within 10 working days. If the concern is about the line manager, it may be raised with the next level manager or with HR.

## 3. Formal grievance

If informal resolution fails, the employee should submit a written grievance to HR using the grievance form. HR acknowledges the grievance within 3 working days and appoints an independent manager to hear it.

## 4. Grievance meeting

A grievance meeting is held within 10 working days of receipt of the written grievance. The employee may be accompanied by a colleague or a trade union representative. The outcome is confirmed in writing within 5 working days after the meeting.

## 5. Grievance appeal

An employee who is not satisfied with the outcome may appeal in writing within 7 working days of receiving the written outcome. The appeal is heard by a senior manager who was not involved earlier, and the appeal decision is final.

## 6. Disciplinary rules

Misconduct includes, for example, repeated lateness, unauthorised absence, failure to follow reasonable instructions and minor breaches of the Code of Conduct. Gross misconduct includes theft or fraud, including falsified expense claims, violence, being under the influence of alcohol or drugs on a vessel or at a terminal, serious breaches of safety rules, and serious breaches of confidentiality.

## 7. Disciplinary stages

The disciplinary stages are:
- Stage 1: a written warning, which remains on file for 6 months.
- Stage 2: a final written warning, which remains on file for 12 months.
- Stage 3: dismissal.

Gross misconduct may lead to dismissal without notice at the first stage. No disciplinary action is taken before an investigation and a disciplinary hearing.

## 8. Suspension

An employee may be suspended on full pay while an allegation of gross misconduct is investigated. Suspension is not a disciplinary sanction and normally lasts no longer than 15 working days.

## 9. Right to be accompanied

At any formal grievance or disciplinary meeting the employee may be accompanied by a colleague or a trade union representative. If the companion is unavailable, the meeting may be postponed once by up to 5 working days.

## 10. Disciplinary appeals

An employee may appeal against any disciplinary sanction in writing within 7 working days of receiving the decision, stating the grounds of appeal.
