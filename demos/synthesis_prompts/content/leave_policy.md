# Harbourline Shipping Ltd - Leave Policy (Shore Staff)

Document HR-POL-01, version 4.2, effective 1 March 2025. Owner: Human Resources.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all shore-based employees of Harbourline Shipping Ltd, including office staff, port staff and technical superintendents. It does not apply to seafarers, whose leave is governed by their collective bargaining agreement. Contractors and agency workers are not covered.

## 2. Leave year

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Entitlements are calculated for each leave year and are pro-rated for employees who join or leave during the year, rounded up to the nearest half day.

## 3. Annual leave entitlement

Annual leave entitlement depends on continuous service at the start of the leave year:
- Less than 3 years of service: 22 working days per year.
- 3 to 5 years of service: 25 working days per year.
- 6 to 10 years of service: 27 working days per year.
- More than 10 years of service: 30 working days per year.

Part-time employees receive the same entitlement pro-rated to their contracted working days. Public holidays are in addition to annual leave.

## 4. Carry-over of unused leave

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March of the following year; any carried-over days not taken by that date are forfeited. Unused leave is not paid out except on termination of employment, when untaken accrued leave is paid at the basic daily rate.

## 5. Requesting annual leave

Leave must be requested through the HR self-service portal. The notice required is at least twice the length of the leave requested; for example, a request for 5 days must be submitted at least 10 days in advance. Requests for more than 10 consecutive working days require the approval of the department head in addition to the line manager. Managers should respond to requests within 5 working days.

## 6. Public holidays

Employees receive the public holidays of the country in which their office is located. An employee who is required to work on a public holiday receives one day of time off in lieu, which must be taken within 3 months.

## 7. Sick leave

Employees who are unable to work because of illness must notify their line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. Absences of up to 3 consecutive calendar days may be self-certified through the portal. Absences longer than 3 consecutive calendar days require a medical certificate.

Company sick pay is paid as follows in any rolling 12-month period:
- Full basic pay for the first 15 working days of sickness absence.
- Half basic pay for the next 30 working days of sickness absence.
- After that, no company sick pay is paid, although statutory sick pay may apply.

A return-to-work meeting with the line manager is required after any absence of more than 5 working days.

## 8. Maternity leave

Employees are entitled to 26 weeks of maternity leave. Employees with at least 1 year of service receive full basic pay for the first 16 weeks and statutory pay for the remaining 10 weeks. Employees must notify HR of the expected week of childbirth at least 15 weeks before it.

## 9. Paternity and partner leave

Employees are entitled to 3 weeks of paternity or partner leave at full basic pay, to be taken within 8 weeks of the birth.

## 10. Adoption leave

Adoption leave mirrors maternity leave: 26 weeks, with full basic pay for the first 16 weeks for employees with at least 1 year of service. The partner of the primary adopter is entitled to 3 weeks of partner leave at full basic pay.

## 11. Compassionate leave

Employees may take up to 5 working days of paid compassionate leave on the death of a close family member (spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling) and up to 2 working days for other relatives. Additional time may be agreed as annual leave or unpaid leave.

## 12. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to 20 working days per leave year may be granted at the discretion of the department head. Unpaid leave is not granted while the employee still has annual leave available, except in exceptional circumstances approved by HR.

## 13. Approval and records

The line manager approves all leave except where this policy requires department-head or HR approval. All leave must be recorded in the HR self-service portal. Questions about this policy should be sent to hr@harbourline.example.
