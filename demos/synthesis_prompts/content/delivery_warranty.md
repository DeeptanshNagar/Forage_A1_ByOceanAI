# Delivery, Returns and Warranty Conditions

Procurement Department, document PROC-DLV-03, valid from 1 January 2025.

## 1. Delivery terms

Marisupply GmbH and Baltic Marine Spares deliver FCA at their warehouse (Hamburg and Gdansk respectively). Oceanic Parts Co delivers DAP to the nominated port agent. Kestrel Engineering delivers EXW from its workshop in Rotterdam. Harbour Tech Ltd delivers DAP to the vessel's port of call within Europe and FCA Singapore for Asian ports.

## 2. Lead times and expedited delivery

The lead time shown in the spares catalogue is the standard lead time in calendar days from order confirmation to dispatch. Expedited dispatch can halve the standard lead time, rounded up to whole days, and carries a surcharge of 25% of the order value, with a minimum surcharge of 150 USD. Expedited dispatch is not available for items with zero stock.

## 3. Delivery to vessels

When parts are delivered on board through a port agent, an agent handling fee of 85 USD per consignment applies, plus 0.5% of the order value for consignments above 10,000 USD. The superintendent must give the port agent at least 3 working days' notice of the vessel's arrival.

## 4. Warranty periods by category

Warranty runs from the date of delivery:
- Engine parts: 12 months.
- Pumps: 18 months.
- Electrical parts: 24 months.
- Navigation equipment: 24 months.
- Safety equipment: 36 months.
- Deck Machinery: 12 months.
- HVAC parts: 18 months.
- Filters: 6 months.

## 5. What voids the warranty

The warranty is void if parts are installed by personnel without the required certification, if the manufacturer's installation instructions are not followed, if parts are used outside their rated operating conditions, or if the original packaging seals of safety equipment are broken before installation.

## 6. Warranty claims

Warranty claims must be submitted within 30 days of discovering the defect, with photographs, the part number, the vessel name and the purchase order number. The supplier decides within 15 working days whether to repair, replace or credit the part. Freight for replacement parts under warranty is paid by the supplier.

## 7. Returns of unused parts

Unused parts in their original packaging may be returned within 60 days of delivery. A restocking fee of 15% of the part price applies, except for Kestrel Engineering, which charges 20%. Custom-made parts and opened filters cannot be returned.

## 8. Damage in transit

Visible damage must be noted on the delivery receipt and reported to the supplier within 48 hours of delivery. Hidden damage must be reported within 7 days.
