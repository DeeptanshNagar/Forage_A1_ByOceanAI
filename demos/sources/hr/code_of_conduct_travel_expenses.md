# Harbourline Shipping Ltd - Code of Conduct and Travel & Expenses Policy

Document HR-POL-02, version 3.0, effective 1 March 2025. Applies to all shore-based employees.

# Part A - Code of Conduct

## A1. Conflicts of interest

Employees must declare any personal, financial or family interest in a supplier, customer, charterer or competitor to their line manager and to Compliance within 10 working days of becoming aware of it. Employees must not take part in any purchasing or chartering decision in which they have a declared interest.

## A2. Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts with a value of up to 50 USD per gift, to a maximum of 150 USD from the same giver in a calendar year. Gifts above 50 USD must be declined or handed to Compliance. Hospitality (meals, events) with a value above 100 USD per person must be approved in advance by the line manager and recorded in the gifts and hospitality register. Cash or cash equivalents, such as vouchers, must never be accepted regardless of value.

## A3. Confidentiality

Commercial information about freight rates, charter parties, crew data and vessel performance is confidential. It must not be shared outside the company without a signed non-disclosure agreement approved by Legal.

## A4. Use of company IT

Company laptops and phones are for business use; limited personal use is allowed. Company data must only be stored on approved systems. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours.

## A5. Alcohol and port visits

Employees visiting vessels or port terminals must not consume alcohol for 8 hours before the visit. Personal protective equipment must be worn on board and on the quay at all times.

# Part B - Travel and Expenses

## B1. Booking travel

All business travel must be booked through the company travel desk at least 7 days before departure, except in emergencies such as urgent vessel attendance. Travel must be approved by the line manager before booking.

## B2. Class of air travel

Flights with a scheduled duration of up to 6 hours must be booked in economy class. Flights longer than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class requires approval from a director and is only permitted for flights longer than 10 hours.

## B3. Hotel limits

Maximum nightly hotel rates, including taxes and excluding breakfast, are:
- Europe: 180 USD per night.
- Asia: 150 USD per night.
- Americas: 200 USD per night.
- Middle East and Africa: 160 USD per night.

Higher rates require approval from the department head before booking.

## B4. Per diem allowances

A daily per diem covers meals and incidental expenses when travelling overnight:
- Europe: 60 USD per day.
- Asia: 45 USD per day.
- Americas: 65 USD per day.
- Middle East and Africa: 50 USD per day.

On the day of departure and the day of return, half of the per diem is paid. Meals provided by a host or included in a conference fee reduce the per diem by 20 USD per meal.

## B5. Use of private cars

Business use of a private car is reimbursed at 0.45 USD per kilometre. Parking and tolls are reimbursed with receipts. Commuting between home and the normal office is not reimbursed.

## B6. Unused leave and travel

Unused annual leave cannot be exchanged for travel allowances or extra per diems within the leave year (1 April to 31 March).

## B7. Expense claims

Expense claims must be submitted through the expenses system within 30 days of the end of the trip. Original receipts are required for every item above 25 USD. Claims are approved by the line manager; claims above 2,000 USD also require department-head approval. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll.

## B8. Late claims

Claims submitted later than 45 days after the end of the trip will not be reimbursed unless the department head approves an exception in writing.

## B9. Breaches

Breaches of this code may lead to disciplinary action under the Grievance and Disciplinary Procedure (HR-POL-03).
